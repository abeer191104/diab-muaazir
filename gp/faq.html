Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is the purpose of this platform?

This platform is designed to securely collect, store, and display insulin pump data. It helps diabetic patients and healthcare providers monitor glucose levels, insulin doses, and device status in real time.

6. What does the carbohydrate calculator do?

The carb calculator helps patients estimate the correct amount of insulin needed based on how many grams of carbohydrates they are planning to eat. This supports more accurate meal-based insulin dosing.

2. Is this platform connected to a real insulin pump?

For the project phase, the platform uses a simulator to generate realistic data such as glucose levels, insulin delivery, battery percentage, and alerts. In future development, it can be integrated with real pumps that support secure communication protocols.

7. How are alerts delivered to the user?

Alerts for abnormal blood sugar levels or device warnings can be sent through email, SMS, or displayed directly in the user interface.

3. How does the platform protect patient data?

All data is encrypted during transmission and while stored in our secure database. This prevents unauthorized access, data modification, or interception by attackers.

8. Is this system intended to replace medical advice?

No. This platform supports diabetes management but does not replace professional medical recommendations. Users should always consult their doctor before making major changes to treatment settings.

4. Who can access the patient’s information?

Patients can view their own data through the patient dashboard. Doctors or family members can be granted access with permission from the patient for remote monitoring.

9. What type of database is used for storing data?

The system uses a secure cloud database such as Firebase or MongoDB to store medical records, device status, and user account information.

5. What is the “Find My Pump” feature used for?

“Find My Pump” allows the user to locate the insulin pump if it is lost or misplaced by showing its last known location or connection status.

10. Can this system detect cyberattacks on the pump?

The platform includes security measures to protect the connection and detect suspicious access behavior. It focuses on preventing unauthorized control and ensuring the integrity of medical data.
