About Us

Welcome to Diab Muaazir, an innovative platform designed to enhance the safety, security, and convenience of diabetic patients who rely on insulin pumps.

Our mission is to create a secure and intelligent web platform that protects sensitive medical data while assisting patients in managing their condition more effectively. We combine strong encryption, user authentication, and real-time tracking features to ensure both patient safety and peace of mind.

As a team of dedicated computer science students, we developed this project as part of our graduation work to address the growing challenges of cybersecurity and device management in healthcare. One of our unique features is the tracking system integrated with the insulin pump, allowing users to locate their device easily if it’s misplaced or lost.

In addition to security and tracking, our platform provides educational resources and awareness materials to help users understand how to manage their condition safely and confidently.

Our vision is to build a future where technology and healthcare work hand in hand, ensuring that every diabetic patient can rely on their devices securely, efficiently, and with confidence.
